Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about EMI, SIP, Nifty 50, Bank Nifty and Finolab tools.

How do I calculate my loan EMI online?

Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure in Finolab's EMI calculator. It applies the standard formula EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the principal, r the monthly rate and n the number of months, and shows your monthly EMI, total interest and total payment.

SIP calculator kaise kaam karta hai? (SIP कैलकुलेटर क्या है?)

SIP calculator aapki monthly investment, expected annual return aur time period ke basis par estimated maturity value batata hai. Returns market par depend karte hain, isliye result sirf ek estimate hai, guarantee nahi.

What are Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty?

Nifty 50 is the NSE benchmark index that tracks 50 large, actively traded Indian companies. Bank Nifty (Nifty Bank) tracks the most liquid and large banking stocks listed on the NSE. Finolab shows live interactive charts for both, along with Sensex and global markets.

Are Finolab calculators free to use?

Yes. All calculators, market charts and guides on Finolab are free, and you do not need to sign up to use them.

Is the market data on Finolab real-time?

Charts are powered by TradingView and quotes may be delayed depending on the exchange. Use the data for research and learning, and confirm prices with your broker before trading.

Can I use Finolab in Hindi?

Yes. Use the EN / हिंदी switch in the top navigation to read the site in English or Hindi.
